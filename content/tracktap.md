TrackTap is the simplest way to track habits. Tap an emoji, log an event, and keep your streaks moving.

![TrackTap hero](/images/tracktap-hero.png)

## Why TrackTap
- One-tap logging with emojis
- Clean streaks and simple insights
- Fast widgets for quick access
- Apple Watch support

# Support

Need help with TrackTap? Reach out and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

<a href="mailto:tracktap@obycode.com">tracktap@obycode.com</a>

When contacting us for support, please include:
- Device model
- iOS version
- App version
- A short description of the issue

## Common questions
- Restoring purchases: open the app and tap Upgrade, then Restore Purchases.
- Widgets not updating: open the app once to refresh, then re-add the widget.

## Privacy
With TrackTap, your data is under your control. We don’t access your data and we don’t collect personal information.

title: "TrackTap Privacy Policy"

Effective date: 2026-01-31

## Summary
TrackTap is designed to work without collecting your personal information. Your habit data stays on your device, and we do not access it.

## Data we collect
We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data or habit data from the app.

## Data sharing
We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

## Third-party services
TrackTap does not include advertising SDKs. Your device may back up app data as part of iOS backups, which are managed by Apple and subject to Apple’s privacy policies.

## Support
If you contact support, we receive the information you send (such as your email address and message). We use it only to respond to your request.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page with a new effective date.

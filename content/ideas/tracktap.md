# TrackTap

![Emoji tracking](/images/emoji-track.png)

A fast, tap-first habit tracker built around emojis, streaks, and quick logging. The goal is to reduce friction to almost zero: view the app orwidget, tap once, and move on. Focus stays on momentum rather than stats.

## Why?
I want something super simple and easy to use. If tracking something takes any effort or brainpower whatsoever, it might break the habit. Reduce as much as possible so users can focus on the habit they are tracking.

## Core ideas
- Emoji == item to track
- Tap == create an event for this item
- Home screen or lock screen widgets for the fastest possible log.
- Watch app and complications for even quicker tracking
